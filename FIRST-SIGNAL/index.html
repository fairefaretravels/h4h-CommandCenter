Mission objective

Launch the station. Build the first campaign. Prove the system.

First Signal is the controlled launch operation for Radio Meltdown and its first featured artist campaign. The objective is not to look busy. It is to establish a repeatable operating system that produces audience growth, press attention, partnerships and revenue.

Executive rule: no public publishing, paid spend, media outreach, contracts or external commitments happen without Executive Director approval.

Executive priorities

  1. Confirm the station experience works end-to-end: website, stream, player and mobile.
  2. Choose the lead artist, track and campaign objective.
  3. Build the campaign calendar around real assets and a realistic launch date.
  4. Assign clear owners, deadlines and success measures.
  5. Review results weekly and adjust based on evidence.
