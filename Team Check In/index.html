What if the problem was never the plan?

In live events, the biggest problems rarely announce themselves. They're small. Someone needs water. A host needs a microphone. Security needs another set of eyes. An artist hasn't checked in. The DJ is missing a song. A camera operator needs a hand.

Someone leaves their post to go find the person who can solve it — and suddenly two positions are uncovered instead of one. That's how chaos starts. Not with one big failure, but with a dozen small ones happening at once, with nobody coordinating them.

Don't leave your post. Ask the team.

That's the whole idea behind TEAM CHECK-IN™ — a simple, mobile-first coordination system that turns event and venue operations into a series of clear questions, one-tap buttons, and live status updates. No giant dashboard. No complicated training. No employee surveillance. Just: check in, do your checklist, stay on your post, ask for help when you need it, and help when you're free.

The checklist is the training

Nobody has to memorize an entire event procedure before doors open. They just answer the next question.

Are you at your assigned position? Yes — ready. No — need help. Then the system moves forward. Another question. Another check. Another confirmation. By the end, the team member knows their job because they just walked through it — not because they sat through a training deck three weeks ago.

When something goes wrong

This is where TEAM CHECK-IN™ earns its keep. Say the host suddenly needs water mid-soundcheck. Traditionally, someone leaves their position, walks the venue, and starts asking around — now they're away from their assignment, the host is still waiting, and somebody else is looking for them.

With TEAM CHECK-IN™, the request becomes a team task instead of a personal errand. The person stays at their post. An available teammate sees the request and taps 🤝 I'll Handle It. Now everyone knows who's on it — a tiny interaction that solves a real operational problem.

🟢 I'm Available
When someone finishes or pauses their primary assignment, they can flag themselves as available — turning your free staff into a mobile support layer instead of people wandering the venue looking for something to do.
🔊 Listen To This
Not everyone processes information the same way, and loud venues make screens hard to read on the move. A one-tap audio readout puts the current question in someone's ear instead of forcing them to squint at a phone.
✅ Status, not surveillance
Team leads see who's ready, who's working, and who needs help — never location history, personal files, or anything that looks like monitoring.

Privacy isn't an afterthought

Today's workplace software raises a legitimate question: how much access should an employer have to an employee's personal phone? TEAM CHECK-IN™ takes the opposite approach. We need to know your work status — not your personal life.

The system is designed around coordination, not surveillance. That means no:

  • Location tracking
  • Access to personal photos
  • Access to personal files
  • Screenshots
  • Invasive phone monitoring
  • Employee ratings

The app should help the team work. It shouldn't become another boss living inside someone's phone.

Small words, said at the right moment

Events get stressful. Things go wrong. People get tired. The best software in the world can't fix a team that's mentally scattered — so TEAM CHECK-IN™ threads small, human moments through the experience. "We got this." "Move together." "Your part matters." "Take a breath. Reset. Handle the next thing."

Small, but intentional. A workplace isn't just a collection of tasks. It's people.
