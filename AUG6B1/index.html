05 / OPERATIONS

What We Fix

TIMING
No More Late Starts

Host, DJ, staff, camera and production need to be ready before the advertised show start.

DOOR
Control The Traffic

Separate entrance and exit when possible. Otherwise use a rope barrier and defined payment/wristband stations.

ROOM
Make The Crowd Look Bigger

Condense seating and bring the audience closer to the stage. Avoid large empty performance areas.

VOTING
Vote At The End

All artists perform first. DJ recap mix. Then the crowd votes so the final artist isn't disadvantaged by early departures.

CONTENT
Capture Every Winner

Winner announcement, interview, photos, crowd reaction and social clips need to be part of the production checklist.

PROMOTION
Build The Crowd Before The Event

Explain what HIT FOR HIT is, what it costs, how voting works and why someone should choose this over another night out.
